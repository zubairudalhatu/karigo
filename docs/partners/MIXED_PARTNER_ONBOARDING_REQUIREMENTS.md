# Mixed Partner Onboarding Requirements

Mixed partners sell products and provide SME services through one Partner Workspace account.

## Typical Examples

- A hardware store selling products and offering installation services.
- A laundry business selling laundry products and offering laundry pickup services.
- A car care business selling accessories and offering mechanic or cleaning services.
- A printing shop selling printed items and offering custom print services.

## Required Profile Information

Mixed partners must complete both product-seller and service-provider details:

- business identity;
- product category;
- service category;
- main branch/location;
- launch city;
- customer-facing description;
- operations contact;
- operating hours;
- payout readiness details for future settlement review.

## Catalogue Requirements

Mixed partners should keep product and service catalogues separate:

- Products use product names, prices, images and stock/availability.
- Services use service names, descriptions, quote notes, coverage areas and readiness status.

Do not force service-only offerings into product catalogue entries.

## Admin Review

Admin should review mixed accounts in two passes:

1. Product readiness: products, pricing, order preparation and pickup.
2. Service readiness: service category, coverage, documents and manual coordination process.

Both areas do not need to launch on the same day. Admin may approve one surface and keep the other inactive until ready.

## Guardrails

- Do not combine product and service payments into unreviewed flows.
- Do not expose service provider private contact details to customers.
- Do not mark regulated service categories launch-ready without separate approval.
- Do not activate payout automation from mixed onboarding.
