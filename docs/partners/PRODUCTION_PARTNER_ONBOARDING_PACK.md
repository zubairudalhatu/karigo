# Production Partner Onboarding Pack

This pack defines the production onboarding process for KariGO partners before wider launch.

Official partner entry point:

```text
https://vendor.karigo.com.ng/register
```

## Supported Partner Types

KariGO supports three partner account types:

| Partner Type | Use Case | Workspace Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product Seller | Restaurants, grocery stores, market sellers and approved merchants selling products. | Product catalogue, orders, profile, documents and settlements visibility. |
| Service Provider | SME service providers offering approved services such as plumbing, cleaning, printing, laundry or professional services. | Services catalogue, profile, documents and SME Services readiness workflow. |
| Both | Partners that sell products and also provide services. | Product and Services catalogue areas. |

## Production Onboarding Stages

1. Partner submits the online registration/application.
2. Admin reviews the application for identity, business fit, city readiness and document completeness.
3. Admin approves, requests changes, rejects or moves duplicate/test records to Trash.
4. Backend links or creates the Partner/Vendor account and activation flow.
5. Partner sets password and signs in to Partner Workspace.
6. Partner completes profile, logo/cover, branch/location and onboarding document upload.
7. Admin reviews uploaded documents and operational readiness.
8. Partner catalogue/services are reviewed for quality and pricing.
9. Partner is marked ready for controlled launch only after Admin signoff.

## Launch Cities

Initial production onboarding should prioritize:

- Kano
- Abuja

Partners outside supported cities may be recorded for future expansion, but they should not be marked launch-ready until Operations approves city coverage.

## Guardrails

- Do not activate unverified partners publicly.
- Do not enable payouts, automated settlement or live promotions from onboarding alone.
- Do not approve regulated services without legal and operations review.
- Do not expose private document URLs, phone numbers, payment secrets or internal notes outside approved Admin surfaces.
- Do not recreate deleted profiles from Partner Workspace. Admin must restore or approve accounts.
