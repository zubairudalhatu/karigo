# Partner Operations Rules

These rules guide KariGO Admin, Operations and Support during production partner onboarding.

## General Rules

- Keep onboarding account-first and Admin-reviewed.
- Keep product sellers, service providers and mixed partners clearly classified.
- Keep Kano and Abuja as the priority supported cities unless leadership approves expansion.
- Keep disabled services disabled until separately approved.
- Keep test/demo records out of production-facing lists.

## Product Seller Rules

- Products must have clear names, prices and customer-facing descriptions.
- Product images should be appropriate and owned or permitted for use.
- Unavailable products should be archived or marked inactive.
- Vendors must understand order preparation and dispatch handoff.

## Service Provider Rules

- Services must describe the service clearly and avoid misleading guarantees.
- Quote-based services should say that KariGO Operations will confirm details.
- Private provider phone/email must not be exposed to customers unless approved.
- Regulated services require separate review.

## Payment And Settlement Rules

- Wallet, Flutterwave, Pay on Delivery and Utilities workflows must follow the current approved launch configuration.
- Do not manually promise automated payouts or refunds unless the system and policy support them.
- Pay on Delivery reconciliation should be tracked operationally.
- Settlement visibility does not equal automatic payout.

## Account Cleanup Rules

- Use Trash before permanent delete.
- Permanent delete is only for duplicate/test records that pass backend safety checks.
- Keep real partner records with operational history retained for audit.
- Partner Workspace must not reactivate missing profiles automatically.

## Support Rules

- Use clear support categories: onboarding, documents, login, catalogue, orders, payments, services.
- Escalate payment, wallet, legal, regulated category and safety issues to the correct owner.
- Do not share secrets, OTPs, credentials, provider keys or private document URLs.
