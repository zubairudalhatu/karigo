# Partner Document Checklist

This checklist defines the document evidence KariGO may request during partner onboarding.

## Core Documents

| Document | Product Seller | Service Provider | Both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner or representative ID | Required | Required | Required |
| Business registration evidence | Recommended | Recommended | Recommended |
| Business location proof | Recommended | Recommended if office/shop exists | Recommended |
| Storefront/workplace photo | Required where applicable | Recommended | Required where applicable |
| Bank/payout account details | Required for settlement readiness | Required if paid through KariGO | Required |
| Work sample or portfolio | Optional | Required where relevant | Required for services |
| Licence/certification/permit | Category-specific | Category-specific | Category-specific |

## Sensitive Document Rules

- Documents must be uploaded only through approved Partner Workspace or Admin workflows.
- Do not request passwords, card PINs, OTPs, secret keys or private wallet credentials.
- Do not paste document links into public channels.
- Do not expose document URLs to Customer App or public website surfaces.
- Admin review notes should separate internal risk notes from applicant-visible feedback.

## Document Review Statuses

Recommended operational labels:

- Submitted
- Needs clearer copy
- Needs replacement
- Accepted for pilot
- Deferred by Operations
- Rejected

## Retention And Cleanup

- Keep real partner documents for audit and compliance according to KariGO policy.
- Move duplicate/test applications to Trash before permanent delete.
- Permanent delete is only for safe duplicate/test records with no protected operational history.
