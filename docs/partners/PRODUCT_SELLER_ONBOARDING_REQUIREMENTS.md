# Product Seller Onboarding Requirements

Use this checklist for restaurants, grocery stores, market sellers and approved product vendors.

## Required Profile Information

- Legal or registered business name.
- Trading/display name.
- Owner or authorized contact full name.
- Phone number and email address.
- Business category.
- Launch city and service area.
- Business address or pickup location.
- Operating days and hours.
- Product preparation or pickup timing.

## Required Documents

- Business owner ID or authorized representative ID.
- Business registration evidence if available.
- Food handling or health compliance evidence where applicable.
- Storefront, kitchen, shop or product photos.
- Bank/payout account details for future settlement review.
- Any category-specific permit requested by KariGO Operations.

## Catalogue Readiness

Product sellers should prepare:

- Product names.
- Clear descriptions.
- Prices in NGN.
- Product images.
- Availability status.
- Preparation time where applicable.
- Category assignment.

## Operational Checks

- Confirm the pickup address is accurate.
- Confirm the vendor can receive and prepare orders during pilot hours.
- Confirm order status workflow is understood.
- Confirm vendor understands Pay on Delivery reconciliation where applicable.
- Confirm no prohibited, unsafe or regulated products are listed without approval.

## Approval Outcome

Admin may approve a Product Seller for controlled launch only when:

- identity and business details are credible;
- required documents are uploaded or approved as deferred by Operations;
- catalogue is clean and customer-facing;
- city/service area is supported;
- vendor understands order handling and support escalation.
