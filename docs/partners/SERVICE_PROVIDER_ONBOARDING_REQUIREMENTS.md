# Service Provider Onboarding Requirements

Use this checklist for SME service providers joining KariGO through Partner Workspace.

## Supported Service Categories

Examples of approved or readiness-reviewed categories include:

- Plumber
- Painter
- Mechanic
- Electrician
- Cleaner
- Carpenter
- AC technician
- Generator repair technician
- Printing
- Car Hire
- Laundry
- Lesson Teacher
- Legal Practitioner
- Rent a Car
- Other approved SME service providers

Health, legal and other regulated services must remain subject to additional review before public activation.

## Required Profile Information

- Provider or business name.
- Primary contact full name.
- Phone number and email address.
- Service category.
- Launch city and service area.
- Operating days and hours.
- Service address or office location, if available.
- Years of experience or business background.
- Service summary and pricing notes.

## Required Documents

- Valid ID for the provider or authorized representative.
- Business registration evidence if available.
- Work sample, portfolio or service evidence.
- Certification, licence or permit where applicable.
- Guarantor or reference details if required by Operations.
- Insurance or safety documentation where relevant.

## Service Catalogue Readiness

Service providers should prepare:

- Service names.
- Clear descriptions.
- Starting price or quote-based pricing note.
- Service coverage areas.
- Estimated response time.
- Service images if helpful.
- Safety notes and exclusions.

## Approval Outcome

Admin may approve a Service Provider for controlled launch only when:

- identity and contact information are credible;
- category is approved for the current launch scope;
- documents or work evidence are acceptable;
- private phone/email exposure is controlled;
- service request handling remains manual and operations-reviewed.
