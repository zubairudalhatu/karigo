# Partner Activation Checklis

- Partner account and capability (Product Seller, Service Provider or Both) approved.
- City, address, branches, operating hours and support contact verified.
- Required documents reviewed and account operational.
- Products/services have valid names, prices, images and availability.
- At least one approved catalogue item exists for each launch category offered.
- Partner can go Online/Offline and receives orders in a controlled drill.
- Accept/reject, preparation/readiness and handoff flow tested.
- Settlement/payout account readiness reviewed; no automatic payout enabled by this task.
- Service pause blocks new Customer activity but preserves catalogue and historical order access.

Activation confirmation: “Your KariGO Partner operations access is active for the approved city and capabilities. Keep availability and catalogue information current during operating hours.”
