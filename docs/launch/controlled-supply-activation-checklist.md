# Controlled supply activation checklist

## Group and identity

- [ ] Existing approved identity selected; no duplicate Captain or Partner created.
- [ ] Kano or Abuja and exact service verified.
- [ ] Group is `DRAFT`, has a conservative maximum, an operating window, and a safe internal note.
- [ ] Member type matches service and capability.
- [ ] Audited add reason recorded without secrets or private documents.

## Captain eligibility

- [ ] Account active, login ready, relevant application approved, and required documents approved.
- [ ] Operational profile active; vehicle data valid where required.
- [ ] Fresh location available when required.
- [ ] No suspension or conflicting active assignment.
- [ ] Correct controlled group membership exists.

## Partner eligibility

- [ ] Account/profile active, application and required documents approved.
- [ ] Product Seller, Service Provider, or mixed capability matches the group.
- [ ] At least one relevant active product or service exists.
- [ ] Partner is not suspended, trashed, or city mismatched.
- [ ] Correct controlled group membership exists.

## Before activation

- [ ] Operations Customer is enabled and excluded from campaigns.
- [ ] Backend, Support, SEV, ownership, payment method, reconciliation, pause control, hours, capacity, and group checks are complete.
- [ ] Every waiver includes reason, Admin audit identity, and future expiry.
- [ ] Group activated through Admin with an audited reason.
- [ ] Member activated through Admin only after blockers are clear.

Activation never changes a launch stage or activates payouts automatically.
