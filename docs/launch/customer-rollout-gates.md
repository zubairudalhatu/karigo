# Customer Rollout Gates

## Operations-only to Invite-only

All must be evidenced: backend healthy, duty coverage assigned, configured supply met, relevant Ride/delivery/order/service drills passed, support channel tested, no unresolved SEV1 and no launch-blocking SEV2. Admin records the final decision and reason.

## Invite-only to Limited public

The cohort has completed representative transactions; assignment/completion, crash, login/session, payment/reconciliation and support performance are acceptable; no critical safety/privacy issue remains; Operations approves expansion. Cohort names are not exposed to non-members.

## Limited public to City-wide

Stable performance is sustained across operating windows; supply and capacity headroom are sufficient; unfulfilled/cancellation rates are acceptable; payment and settlement exceptions are controlled; support backlog is within target; no blocking incident is open; Executive/Operations approval is recorded.

## Customer messages

- Invite required: “This KariGO service is currently available to invited customers.”
- At capacity: “KariGO is currently at capacity in your area. Please try again shortly.”
- Closed: use the configured city/service closed message.
- Paused: “This KariGO service is temporarily paused. Existing activity remains available.”

No stage promotes automatically. Nationwide availability must not be implied.
