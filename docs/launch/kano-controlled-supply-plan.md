# Kano controlled supply plan

## Current recommendation

`NOT_READY — SUPPLY_REQUIRED` until live, owner-approved controlled records and drill evidence exist. Configured targets alone are not evidence and are not sufficient for Customer invitations.

## Drill minimums

- One enabled controlled Ride Captain.
- One enabled controlled Delivery Captain.
- One enabled controlled Product Seller or mixed Partner.
- One enabled controlled Service Provider or mixed Partner.
- One enabled, campaign-excluded Kano Operations Customer.

## Owner preparation

1. Select already-approved Kano accounts; do not create duplicate Captain or Partner records.
2. Create separate Kano groups per service in `DRAFT` with conservative maximums and an operating window.
3. Add existing account IDs, review every eligibility blocker, and record an audited reason.
4. Activate only eligible members, then activate the reusable Kano Operations Customer.
5. Configure operating hours and conservative capacity in the existing launch controls.
6. Complete the city/service Operations-only checklist and assign Operations, Support, and reconciliation owners.

## Recommended first drill

Run a Kano Ride end-to-end drill first with one controlled Customer and one controlled Ride Captain. Manual assignment must remain enabled and automatic matching disabled. If any critical failure occurs, pause new activity, preserve the active Ride, create the appropriate incident/support record, and return the service to `OFF` after the active work is safe.

Kano is drill ready only after all four supply minimums exist. It remains not ready for invitations or broader access until a later owner decision and later task.
