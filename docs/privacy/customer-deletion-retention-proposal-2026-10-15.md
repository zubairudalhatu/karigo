# Customer deletion retention proposal — business/legal approval required

Prepared 15 October 2026. This is a proposal, not an approved legal retention schedule. It describes the local version-1 implementation and its limits. No real Customer has been erased and no Production schema, readiness or provider setting was changed.

## Governing decisions

The owner approved a maximum 30-day completion timeframe and erasure of ordinary personal data. Financial/legal records may remain only on a legitimate documented basis, with unnecessary identity removed. **All exact retention durations and all cases requiring identifiable Customer data are LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** No statutory period is assumed here. Apple requires deletion of the account and associated personal data/content that need not legally remain; deactivation alone is insufficient: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion.

A = DELETE; B = ANONYMIZE/PSEUDONYMIZE; C = RETAIN WITH DOCUMENTED JUSTIFICATION. B does not imply that retained internal IDs are legally anonymous: historical linkage can remain pseudonymous. C is conditional, not a claim that a legal duty exists for every listed record.

## Storage, processors and backups

| Boundary | Required cleanup / retained-data decision |
|---|---|
| Profile assets | Current Customer photo selection persists an inline data URI; erasing User.profilePhotoUrl removes that asset from the live database. Legacy owned remote objects must be verified and removed using an approved storage path. Arbitrary remote URLs are never fetched/deleted. Admin external-cleanup evidence is mandatory. |
| GCS/private support/application assets | No broad bucket deletion. Customer-owned objects require verified ownership and cleanup evidence. Mixed-role document objects remain blocked pending scope review. No GCS/IAM mutation in this task. |
| Expo push/APNs | Local device registration removed and future registration rejected. Do not claim a general provider-side erase API exists. Verify processor retention documentation and remove any separately held Customer mapping where supported. |
| Paybeta / Flutterwave | Financial processor-held records may require legitimate retention; obtain applicable contractual/legal basis, duration and deletion/minimization capability. KariGO raw PII/provider JSON is removed locally. No purchase, token regeneration or processor financial deletion is part of cleanup. |
| Agora | Local ride/call association removed. Provider logs/transport identifiers require documented processor scope/retention. Do not invent an API that erases all Agora records. |
| Resend | Completion email is sent once using existing sender/Reply-To, without customer name or marketing/tracking content. KariGO email is then erased; provider ID retained. Provider email retention/deletion capability requires review. Cancelling a scheduled email is not erasure of a sent email. An ambiguous send is never automatically repeated. |
| GA4 / Google Mobile Ads and monitoring | Audit approved collection/processor identifiers and applicable removal/retention mechanisms; do not claim global anonymous status without evidence. No analytics/AdMob settings changed. No new identifiers introduced. |
| Backups/logs | Define restricted backup retention and restoration procedure; reapply deletion tombstones before restored data becomes operational. Exact periods are LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED. Application erasure does not claim instant physical erasure of historical backups or all processor logs. |

## Required decisions before Production completion is authorized

1. Approve exactly which minimized accounting/audit facts have a legitimate retention basis; specify durations and disposal responsibility. Cases needing identifiable data require a separate hold plan and cannot be marked completed by this path.
2. Approve mixed-role/application cleanup, private-file ownership and processor/backup scope. Until then those accounts remain blocked, with operational escalation before the 30-day deadline.
3. Assign daily queue review, deadline escalation and completion-notice reconciliation ownership. The implementation records deadlines/overdue status; it does not guarantee staffing or silently run old requests.
4. Approve coordinated backend/schema/Admin/site/iOS rollout and test with dedicated non-Production fixtures. Existing Android/Web clients require a receipt/confirmation compatibility check before rollout; the Android UI has not been changed or published here.

## Privacy-policy impact — proposed wording only

`apps/website/app/privacy/page.tsx`, section **Retention and deletion**, first paragraph currently describes deactivation/restricted access. Business/legal review should replace it with wording covering permanent deletion, up to 30 days, saved receipt/completion notice, minimization and specific lawful retention exceptions. Processor/private-document and backup descriptions must agree with the approved matrix. The Privacy Policy was **not rewritten** in this task.

Suggested draft, subject to approval: “You can request permanent Customer account deletion from Profile → Privacy & security → Delete account. Account access is disabled while the request is processed, which may take up to 30 days. We delete or anonymize ordinary personal data and associated content. Limited financial, legal or dispute records may be retained only where required by law or another documented legitimate obligation, with unnecessary personal information removed. Your saved deletion receipt shows progress; where an email address is available we send one completion confirmation before removing it from operational account data.”

This wording must not be published as a promise of a live erasure feature before the coordinated implementation is deployed and its legal/provider scope is approved.
