## Safe operations and failure handling

Only authorized operations/finance approvers may complete version-1 Customer erasure. Required inputs are DELETE_CUSTOMER_DATA, a reviewed no-identifiable-hold confirmation, opaque retention-basis evidence and verified external-cleanup evidence. Do not enter customer identity/secrets into evidence references. Active work, nonzero wallet/loyalty values, pending payments/utilities, disputes/refunds, in-flight receipt mail or compensation conflicts block completion. Resolve obligations through their own separately approved workflows; deletion itself does not move money.

If cleanup fails, preserve evidence and retry only the cleanup stage after diagnosis. If notice submission is ambiguous, never resend automatically or fabricate a provider ID. A matching existing email can be reconciled read-only; otherwise keep incomplete and escalate. Database/receipt errors do not expose contact, private capability or provider responses. Completion contact removal cannot be bypassed by setting a status from a legacy Admin path.

Version-0 Customer and complete-account requests containing Customer data cannot use the old deactivation-only completion path. They require separately reviewed remediation. Multi-role/application cleanup remains blocked rather than silently completing with retained ordinary application PII.
