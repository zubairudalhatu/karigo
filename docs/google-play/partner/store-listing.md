## Full description

KariGO Partner helps approved Product Sellers and Service Providers manage their KariGO business activity from Android.

Partners can use the capabilities approved for their account: manage product listings, maintain service listings, review orders, control business availability, view earnings and settlement records, update business information and upload required onboarding materials. Mixed partners can work with both products and services from one account.

Availability depends on approval, document status, supported service areas and KariGO operational controls. Settlement information is informational until confirmed by KariGO's backend and does not represent automatic payout.

The app includes Privacy Policy, Terms, support and a Partner-access deletion request.

## Release notes

KariGO Partner 1.0 provides production mobile operations for approved Product Sellers, Service Providers and mixed partners in supported areas of Kano and Abuja.
