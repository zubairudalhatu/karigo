## Method and Google Play rule

Google Play treats an off-device transfer as collection. It treats a transfer to a third party as sharing unless an exception applies. The service-provider exception is limited to a provider processing on the developer's behalf and instructions. A provider can qualify for one payload and act independently for separate telemetry. A user-initiated transfer can have a different exception, but it must be assessed for the specific action.

Evidence priority was: KariGO account record, incorporated DPA, service terms, privacy policy, provider SDK disclosure, then implementation. Public documents do not prove which online terms the Zamkah account accepted unless account use or the terms themselves establish incorporation.
