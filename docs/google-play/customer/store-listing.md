## Full description

KariGO helps customers access supported local services from one account in Kano and Abuja.

Request a KariGO Ride with map-based pickup and destination selection, review route and fare information, and follow the active request. Browse available local sellers, manage orders and addresses, track SME Services requests, view wallet activity and reach account support.

Availability depends on your location, operating hours, approved partners and available Captains. Payment and service options shown in the app reflect current KariGO configuration. KariGO verifies payment and operational status on its servers before completing a transaction or assignment.

Account controls include profile management, Privacy Policy, Terms, support and a deletion-request process.

## Release notes

KariGO 1.0 introduces the production Customer experience for supported areas in Kano and Abuja, including Ride requests, local orders, services, wallet visibility and account controls.
