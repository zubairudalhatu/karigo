## Full description

KariGO Captain is the operational app for approved Delivery Captains and Ride Captains in supported areas of Kano and Abuja.

Approved Captains can manage Delivery and Ride availability, view the live operational map, receive assigned work, follow pickup and completion steps, review delivery history and see earnings records. Dual-mode Captains can control each approved mode while KariGO keeps one shared current-work state.

Access depends on application approval, document review, operational activation and service availability. The app does not guarantee assignments or earnings. Location is used while the Captain is online or handling active work so KariGO can coordinate service safely; background location is not requested.

Profile, support, Privacy Policy, Terms and account-deactivation controls are included.

## Release notes

KariGO Captain 1.0 provides the production operational experience for approved Delivery and Ride Captains, with map, availability, assignments, history, earnings and account controls.
