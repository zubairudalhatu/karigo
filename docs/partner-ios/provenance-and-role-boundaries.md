Not an Apple reply. No submission or review conversation was changed.

Customer is the service recipient requesting/managing KariGO services. Captain is the approved driver/courier performing assignments, with driver onboarding, availability, work milestones and accepted-work background tracking. Partner is the merchant/business operator managing its own commercial presence, catalogue/service listings, fulfilment visibility, verification documents and settlement readiness.

Partner has separate principal screens and onboarding/permissions/workflows. Its mobile orders remain read-only pending P2.
