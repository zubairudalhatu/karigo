# Internal Partner role/provenance evidence

Not an Apple reply. No submission or review conversation was changed.

Customer is the service recipient requesting/managing KariGO services. Captain is the approved driver/courier performing assignments, with driver onboarding, availability, work milestones and accepted-work background tracking. Partner is the merchant/business operator managing its own commercial presence, catalogue/service listings, fulfilment visibility, verification documents and settlement readiness.

Partner has separate principal screens and onboarding/permissions/workflows. P2 adds its own merchant order operations: accept/reject new paid orders, record a structured business reason, start preparation and mark ready for courier pickup. Confirmation, current-server checks, ownership/approval enforcement and existing audit history protect each action. Courier handoff/dispatch/completion, Customer cancellation and administrative financial operations are not Partner controls. Historical orders remain read-only. No device location, microphone, camera capture, contact, Bluetooth or background-location permission is required. User-selected images/documents and opt-in merchant push serve Partner operations. Backend ownership and approval checks enforce access; UI separation is not the security boundary.

The Partner workspace deliberately reuses first-party `@karigo/config`, `@karigo/shared-types`, KariGO branding and backend/platform infrastructure. React Native, Expo and other SDKs supply technical capabilities; they do not generate KariGO workflows. No overlap percentage is claimed. Shared infrastructure does not make these three roles interchangeable content additions; consolidation would require a multi-role product redesign.

Management previously confirmed custom development, no commercial template/white-label/app-generation service or purchased application source/content library; repository control through Zubairu Dalhatu Malami's GitHub account and development/maintenance/release management by Zamkah Technologies Limited.
